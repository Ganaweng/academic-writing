# Output Formats

Use the smallest format that makes the decision auditable.

## Single term or two-way comparison

Lead with:

> **Recommendation:** Use “X” in this sentence.

Then provide:

| Candidate | Exact meaning here | Field/venue fit | Risk | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| X | … | … | … | Preferred |
| Y | … | … | … | Use only when … |

End with one final sentence ready to paste into the manuscript.

## Phrase or sentence validation

Return:

1. **Verdict** — correct, conditionally correct, or revise.
2. **Recommended sentence** — preserve the original claim strength.
3. **Material changes** — explain only technically meaningful edits.
4. **Ledger entry** — include when the decision will recur.

Do not enumerate trivial punctuation or article corrections unless the user asks
for a teaching explanation.

## Paragraph or subsection polishing

Return in this order:

1. **Revised passage**
2. **Terminology decisions**
3. **Claims needing evidence**
4. **Updated terminology ledger**

Separate terminology problems from broader logical or scientific problems. Do
not hide an unsupported claim behind fluent English.

## Full-manuscript audit

Use an issue table:

| Priority | Current forms | Recommended form | Locations | Reason/action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Critical | … | … | … | Meaning or implementation conflict |
| Major | … | … | … | Field convention or claim-strength issue |
| Minor | … | … | … | Capitalization, hyphenation, or style |

Then provide:

- a locked terminology ledger;
- abbreviation and first-use audit;
- figure/table/equation consistency issues;
- unresolved author decisions;
- revised text only for the scope requested.

## Confidence labels

Use confidence labels only when uncertainty matters:

- **High** — definition or dominant field convention is clear.
- **Medium** — both forms occur, but one better fits the local sense.
- **Low** — evidence is sparse, author-defined, or implementation-dependent.

State what additional evidence would resolve a low-confidence decision.
